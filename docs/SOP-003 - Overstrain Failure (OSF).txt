Title: Handling Torque Spikes and Motor Overstrain
Applies To: Primary Drive Motors
Symptoms:

Sudden spikes in Torque (exceeding 60 Nm) combined with elevated Tool Wear.

Rotational speed drops sharply despite constant power delivery.
Root Cause: Tool binding in the workpiece or attempting to process material that exceeds the machine's density specifications.
Immediate Mitigation Steps:

Trigger an immediate hard stop of the rotational drive to prevent motor winding burnout.

Do not attempt to reverse the motor electronically under load.

Manually release the tension on the workpiece using the mechanical override lever.

Verify the material density matches the batch specifications before restarting.
