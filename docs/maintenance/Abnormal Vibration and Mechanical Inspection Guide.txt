DOCUMENT ID: MNT-005
TITLE: Abnormal Vibration and Mechanical Inspection Guide
APPLIES TO: Drive motors, spindles, tool holders, and workpiece interfaces
SEVERITY: High
RELATED FAILURE CODES: OSF, TWF, RNF

1. PURPOSE
Provide a supporting inspection path for abnormal mechanical behavior that may not be fully explained by torque alone.

2. INSPECTION AREAS
- Tool holder and chuck.
- Spindle and drive interface.
- Workpiece clamping.
- Guards and mounts.
- Visible signs of looseness, impact, or obstruction.

3. RELATION TO OTHER SIGNALS
Abnormal mechanical vibration combined with a torque spike or RPM drop should be investigated alongside OSF guidance.

4. ESCALATION
Escalate repeated abnormal vibration, suspected structural damage, or any condition that cannot be safely inspected during normal maintenance access.
