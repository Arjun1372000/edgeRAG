DOCUMENT ID: DIA-002
TITLE: Torque Trend and Mechanical Loading Diagnostics
APPLIES TO: Primary drive motors, tooling, and workpiece interface
SEVERITY: High
RELATED FAILURE CODES: OSF, TWF, RNF

1. PURPOSE
Provide a diagnostic method for abnormal torque behavior and distinguish gradual tool degradation from sudden mechanical overstrain.

2. INDICATORS
- Gradual torque increase while maintaining rotational speed may indicate tool degradation.
- A sudden torque spike above 60 Nm accompanied by a sharp RPM drop should be investigated as possible overstrain.
- Persistent high torque after tool replacement requires further mechanical investigation.

3. DIAGNOSTIC SEQUENCE
1. Review the torque trend for the previous operating interval.
2. Determine whether the torque rise was gradual or abrupt.
3. Compare torque behavior with Rotational Speed.
4. Check Tool Wear and recent tool-change history.
5. Inspect the workpiece/tool interface for binding.
6. Verify that the processed material matches the production specification.

4. DECISION GUIDANCE
Gradual torque growth with high Tool Wear favors investigation under SOP-002. Sudden torque spikes with rotational-speed collapse favor SOP-003. If neither pattern is clear, use the general investigation procedure.

5. SAFETY
Do not manually access the work envelope while motion can occur. Follow machine stop and isolation procedures before physical inspection.
