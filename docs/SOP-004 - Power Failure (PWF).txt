DOCUMENT ID: SOP-004
TITLE: Power Delivery and Voltage Anomaly Response Procedure
FAILURE CODE: PWF
APPLIES TO: Factory electrical grid and machine power supplies
SEVERITY: Critical

1. PURPOSE
Provide a standard response to abnormal machine power delivery and voltage conditions while minimizing electrical and equipment hazards.

2. FAILURE DESCRIPTION
PWF is associated with abnormal electrical power delivery to the machine. Power can be inferred from Torque × Rotational Speed and may become abnormally low or critically high during an electrical fault.

3. SYMPTOMS / TRIGGER CONDITIONS
- The product of Torque and Rotational Speed (Power) drops below 3500 W.
- Power spikes critically above 9000 W.
- Machine operation may become unstable or stop unexpectedly.
- Electrical protection devices may indicate a fault condition.

4. LIKELY ROOT CAUSES
- Phase loss in the main factory power supply.
- Blown inverter fuses.
- Motor short circuits.
- Other abnormal conditions in the machine power-delivery path.

5. IMMEDIATE MITIGATION
1. Step away from the machine casing to prevent potential arc-flash or grounding hazards.
2. Isolate the machine from the main grid using the wall-mounted breaker.
3. Prevent restart while the electrical fault is being investigated.

6. DIAGNOSTIC CHECKS
- Verify machine power and rotational-speed readings.
- Check the state of electrical protection devices.
- Maintenance engineers should perform a continuity check on inverter fuses.
- Inspect available electrical-fault indicators according to site procedure.

7. ESCALATION CONDITION
Escalate to qualified electrical maintenance personnel for suspected phase loss, inverter-fuse failure, motor short circuit, or any condition involving the factory electrical supply.

8. RESTART CONDITIONS
Do not restart the machine until the factory floor voltage has been verified as stable by the electrical team and the identified fault has been cleared.

9. SAFETY NOTES
Electrical isolation and diagnostics must be performed only by authorized, qualified personnel using applicable lockout/tagout and electrical-safety procedures. Do not open energized electrical equipment.

10. RELATED PARAMETERS
Torque, Rotational Speed, calculated Power, line voltage, electrical protection state, inverter-fuse state.
