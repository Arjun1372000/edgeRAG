DOCUMENT ID: MNT-003
TITLE: Inverter and Motor Electrical Maintenance Guide
APPLIES TO: Motor drives, inverter systems, and machine power electronics
SEVERITY: Critical
RELATED FAILURE CODES: PWF

1. PURPOSE
Provide supporting maintenance information for electrical conditions associated with abnormal power delivery.

2. MAINTENANCE CHECKS
- Inspect inverter-fuse condition through the approved maintenance process.
- Review electrical fault indicators.
- Check for evidence of phase loss.
- Verify motor connection integrity according to site procedure.
- Inspect for signs of overheating or electrical damage.

3. RESTRICTION
Electrical work must be performed only by qualified personnel after the required isolation procedure has been completed.

4. POST-MAINTENANCE
Confirm stable factory supply and cleared fault indicators before controlled restart.
