Title: Mitigation Protocol for Heat Dissipation Anomalies
Applies To: CNC Milling and Assembly Motors
Symptoms:

Air Temperature and Process Temperature differential falls below critical thresholds.

Sustained Process Temperature exceeding 310 K.
Root Cause: Failure of the internal liquid cooling system or blocked ventilation fans, leading to thermal expansion and motor stall.
Immediate Mitigation Steps:

Do not power down immediately; reduce rotational speed to idle (below 1000 RPM) to allow residual cooling.

Engage the secondary backup coolant pump manually.

Check the thermal exhaust vents for physical blockages.

If Process Temperature does not drop within 2 minutes, trigger an emergency electrical disconnect.
