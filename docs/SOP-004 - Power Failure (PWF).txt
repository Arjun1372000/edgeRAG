Title: Power Delivery and Voltage Anomaly Protocols
Applies To: Factory Electrical Grid and Machine Power Supplies
Symptoms:

The product of Torque and Rotational Speed (Power) drops below 3500 W or spikes critically above 9000 W.
Root Cause: Phase loss in the main factory power supply, blown inverter fuses, or motor short circuits.
Immediate Mitigation Steps:

Step away from the machine casing to prevent potential arc flash or grounding hazards.

Isolate the machine from the main grid using the wall-mounted breaker.

Maintenance engineers must perform a continuity check on the inverter fuses.

Do not restart the machine until the factory floor voltage has been verified as stable by the electrical team.
