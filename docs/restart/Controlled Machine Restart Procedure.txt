DOCUMENT ID: RST-001
TITLE: Controlled Machine Restart and Return-to-Service Procedure
APPLIES TO: Automated assembly and CNC machine systems
SEVERITY: High
RELATED FAILURE CODES: HDF, TWF, PWF, OSF, RNF

1. PURPOSE
Define a generic sequence for returning a machine to service after a maintenance or failure event.

2. PRE-RESTART CONDITIONS
- Failure cause or disposition has been recorded.
- Required maintenance has been completed.
- Guards and safety controls are restored.
- No unresolved safety-critical alarm remains.
- Relevant sensor values are plausible.

3. CONTROLLED RESTART
1. Clear the work area.
2. Verify personnel are in safe positions.
3. Restore energy according to the approved site procedure.
4. Start at a controlled operating state.
5. Observe temperature, torque, RPM, tool wear, and relevant alarms.
6. Increase operating load only after stable behavior is confirmed.

4. FAILURE TO STABILIZE
If abnormal behavior returns, stop the restart sequence and escalate rather than repeatedly cycling the machine.
