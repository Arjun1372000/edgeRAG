DOCUMENT ID: RST-002
TITLE: Post-Failure Verification and Restart Checklist
APPLIES TO: Automated assembly and CNC machine systems
SEVERITY: High
RELATED FAILURE CODES: HDF, TWF, PWF, OSF, RNF

1. PURPOSE
Provide a post-maintenance checklist to support a controlled return to production.

2. CHECKLIST
- Confirm the documented failure mode or unclassified disposition.
- Confirm corrective action has been recorded.
- Verify relevant maintenance components.
- Verify sensor readings are current and plausible.
- Verify machine alarms are cleared or understood.
- Verify guards/interlocks are functional.
- Confirm the responsible operator or maintenance engineer authorizes restart.

3. CONDITION-SPECIFIC VERIFICATION
HDF: confirm thermal behavior stabilizes.
TWF: confirm replacement tool and tool-wear state.
PWF: confirm factory voltage and electrical fault clearance.
OSF: confirm mechanical loading is normal.
UNCLASSIFIED: confirm the failure is understood sufficiently for safe operation.

4. ESCALATION
Do not release the machine to full production when any required verification item fails.
