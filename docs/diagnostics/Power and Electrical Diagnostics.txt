DOCUMENT ID: DIA-003
TITLE: Power Delivery and Electrical Fault Diagnostics
APPLIES TO: Machine drives, inverter systems, and factory electrical supply
SEVERITY: Critical
RELATED FAILURE CODES: PWF, RNF

1. PURPOSE
Provide an electrical diagnostic sequence for abnormal inferred power or machine power delivery.

2. POWER CHECK
Calculate or verify inferred machine power using Torque × Rotational Speed according to the system's configured units. Values below 3500 W or above 9000 W should be investigated against the applicable failure procedure.

3. DIAGNOSTIC SEQUENCE
1. Verify current Torque and Rotational Speed readings.
2. Confirm that the readings are plausible and current.
3. Review machine electrical alarm indicators.
4. Check the state of electrical protection devices.
5. Inspect inverter-fuse condition through the approved maintenance process.
6. Determine whether a phase-loss or supply-voltage issue is suspected.

4. DECISION GUIDANCE
When the machine exhibits a confirmed electrical anomaly, use SOP-004 and the electrical isolation procedure. Do not diagnose an energized fault by exposing maintenance personnel to live equipment.

5. ESCALATION
Electrical diagnosis must be escalated to qualified personnel when factory supply, inverter, fuse, or motor electrical integrity is involved.
