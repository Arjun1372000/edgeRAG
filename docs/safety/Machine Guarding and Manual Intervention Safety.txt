DOCUMENT ID: SAF-003
TITLE: Machine Guarding and Manual Intervention Safety
APPLIES TO: CNC machines, cutting tools, drive systems
SEVERITY: High
RELATED FAILURE CODES: TWF, OSF

1. PURPOSE
Define general precautions for inspection and manual intervention around moving machinery.

2. RULES
- Do not enter the work envelope while motion is possible.
- Use the approved stop and isolation sequence before manual inspection.
- Do not attempt to remove a bound workpiece under machine power.
- Restore guards and interlocks before controlled restart.

3. TOOL CHANGE
Use the approved spindle lock and tool-release procedure. Confirm the machine cannot unexpectedly move before entering the cutting area.

4. ESCALATION
Any damaged guard, interlock, spindle lock, or mechanical release system must be escalated before restart.
