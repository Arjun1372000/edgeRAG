Title: Routine and Emergency Tool Replacement Guidelines
Applies To: Drill Bits, Milling Cutters, and Lathe Inserts
Symptoms:

Tool Wear metric exceeds 200 minutes of continuous operation.

Gradual, unexplained increases in Torque required to maintain Rotational Speed.
Root Cause: Physical degradation, dulling, or micro-fracturing of the cutting implement due to prolonged material friction.
Immediate Mitigation Steps:

Halt the current feed cycle and retract the tool arm from the material.

Lock the spindle and initiate the automated tool-release sequence.

Inspect the chuck for metal shavings or debris before inserting the replacement tool.

Reset the Tool Wear timer to 0 in the edge monitoring system before resuming the batch.
