DOCUMENT ID: SOP-003
TITLE: Overstrain Failure (OSF) Detection and Motor Protection Procedure
FAILURE CODE: OSF
APPLIES TO: Primary drive motors
SEVERITY: High

1. PURPOSE
Provide a standard response to torque overload and motor overstrain conditions in order to prevent continued mechanical or electrical stress.

2. FAILURE DESCRIPTION
OSF is associated with excessive mechanical load on the drive system. A sustained or sudden torque increase can indicate tool binding, excessive material load, or another condition that prevents the motor from maintaining normal rotation.

3. SYMPTOMS / TRIGGER CONDITIONS
- Sudden Torque spikes exceeding 60 Nm combined with elevated Tool Wear.
- Rotational Speed drops sharply despite constant power delivery.
- The drive may struggle to maintain normal speed under load.
- Abnormal resistance or binding may be observed at the workpiece/tool interface.

4. LIKELY ROOT CAUSES
- Tool binding in the workpiece.
- Processing material that exceeds the machine's density specifications.
- Excessive cutting load or abnormal mechanical resistance.

5. IMMEDIATE MITIGATION
1. Trigger an immediate hard stop of the rotational drive to prevent motor winding burnout.
2. Do not attempt to reverse the motor electronically under load.
3. Manually release the tension on the workpiece using the mechanical override lever.
4. Inspect the workpiece/tool interface for binding or abnormal loading.

6. DIAGNOSTIC CHECKS
- Review the torque trend immediately before the failure.
- Confirm the Rotational Speed response to the torque increase.
- Check Tool Wear and tool condition.
- Verify that the material density matches the batch specification.
- Inspect for physical binding, obstruction, or mechanical resistance.

7. ESCALATION CONDITION
Escalate to maintenance engineering if the drive remains overloaded after the obstruction/load condition is removed, if abnormal mechanical resistance persists, or if motor protection has been triggered repeatedly.

8. RESTART CONDITIONS
Verify that the source of overload has been removed and that the workpiece/material matches batch specifications before restarting the machine.

9. SAFETY NOTES
Do not enter the machine work area until the drive is stopped and all required isolation procedures have been completed. Never defeat motor-protection or guarding systems.

10. RELATED PARAMETERS
Torque, Rotational Speed, Tool Wear, material specification, drive state.
