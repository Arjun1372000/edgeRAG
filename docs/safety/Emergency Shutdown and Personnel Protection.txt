DOCUMENT ID: SAF-002
TITLE: Emergency Shutdown and Personnel Protection Guidance
APPLIES TO: Automated assembly and CNC machine systems
SEVERITY: Critical
RELATED FAILURE CODES: HDF, OSF, PWF, RNF

1. PURPOSE
Provide generic personnel-protection guidance when an abnormal machine condition creates immediate equipment or safety risk.

2. IMMEDIATE PRIORITY
Personnel safety takes precedence over production continuity.

3. APPLY EMERGENCY RESPONSE WHEN
- A machine condition is rapidly worsening.
- Continued motion creates a credible personnel hazard.
- Electrical hazards are suspected.
- The approved SOP explicitly requires emergency isolation.

4. RESPONSE
Use the machine's approved emergency stop or isolation mechanism as appropriate to the situation. Keep personnel outside hazardous zones and follow site emergency procedures.

5. POST-EVENT
Record the event, preserve telemetry if possible without entering an unsafe area, and escalate to the responsible maintenance/safety team.
