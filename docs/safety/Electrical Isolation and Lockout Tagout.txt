DOCUMENT ID: SAF-001
TITLE: Electrical Isolation and Lockout/Tagout Procedure
APPLIES TO: Industrial machine electrical systems
SEVERITY: Critical
RELATED FAILURE CODES: PWF, HDF, OSF

1. PURPOSE
Provide a generic site-level reference for safely isolating machinery before electrical or mechanical maintenance.

2. WHEN TO APPLY
Apply whenever inspection requires access to energized or potentially energized equipment, or when an electrical fault is suspected.

3. BASIC SEQUENCE
1. Stop the machine using the approved control method when conditions permit.
2. Identify all energy sources relevant to the machine.
3. Isolate the machine using the approved disconnect.
4. Apply the site's lockout/tagout controls.
5. Verify absence of hazardous energy using the site's approved method.
6. Only authorized personnel may proceed with maintenance.

4. IMPORTANT
Do not open energized electrical equipment. Arc-flash, grounding, stored-energy, and unexpected-motion hazards must be assessed by qualified personnel.

5. RELEASE
Remove isolation controls only after maintenance is complete, guards are restored, personnel are clear, and the authorized person confirms readiness for controlled restart.
