DOCUMENT ID: MNT-004
TITLE: Preventive Maintenance Schedule Reference
APPLIES TO: Automated assembly and CNC machine systems
SEVERITY: Medium
RELATED FAILURE CODES: HDF, TWF, PWF, OSF, RNF

1. PURPOSE
Provide a general reference for maintaining machine systems before failures occur.

2. MAINTENANCE AREAS
- Cooling system inspection.
- Tool and spindle inspection.
- Drive and mechanical interface inspection.
- Electrical protection and inverter inspection.
- Sensor and telemetry integrity review.
- Safety guarding and interlock verification.

3. CONDITION-BASED FOLLOW-UP
Repeated alerts from the edge monitoring system should feed into maintenance planning even when each individual event does not require an emergency shutdown.

4. RECORD KEEPING
Maintenance history should record date, asset, observed condition, action taken, parts replaced, and restart verification.
