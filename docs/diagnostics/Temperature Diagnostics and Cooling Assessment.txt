DOCUMENT ID: DIA-001
TITLE: Temperature Diagnostics and Cooling System Assessment
APPLIES TO: CNC milling and automated assembly motors
SEVERITY: High
RELATED FAILURE CODES: HDF, RNF

1. PURPOSE
Provide a diagnostic sequence for abnormal temperature behavior before selecting a maintenance action.

2. NORMAL OBSERVATION
Review Air Temperature and Process Temperature together. A sustained increase in Process Temperature under otherwise stable operating conditions can indicate inadequate heat removal.

3. DIAGNOSTIC SEQUENCE
1. Confirm that both temperature readings are changing plausibly.
2. Compare the Process Temperature against the machine operating envelope.
3. Check whether temperature continues increasing under load.
4. Confirm that the primary cooling pump is running.
5. Check secondary or backup cooling availability.
6. Inspect ventilation paths and exhaust openings.
7. Review whether the machine recently experienced an abnormal load or speed condition.

4. INTERPRETATION
A Process Temperature above 310 K combined with continued thermal rise should be investigated against SOP-001. A temperature sensor that disagrees sharply with other telemetry should be treated as a possible instrumentation issue.

5. ESCALATION
Escalate when temperature cannot be stabilized, sensor readings are inconsistent, or cooling hardware shows evidence of failure.

6. REQUIRED RECORDS
Record timestamp, Air Temperature, Process Temperature, rotational speed, operating load, pump state, and observations made during inspection.
